Create a simple n2p proposal slide illustration on a pure white background, wide 16:9. Keep it close to a clean business diagram, not a highly polished infographic. Use black and dark gray linework, simple gray fills, and one or two very small n2p yellow accents only.

Strict style constraints: no shadows of any kind, no perspective, no tilt, no skew, no angled screens, no isometric view, no 3D depth, no glossy surfaces, no gradients, no decorative micro-details. Everything must be flat, front-facing, orthographic, and simple.
