## Prompt

Purpose: 速いAIモックがそのままでは提案価値にならず、案件理解と意図の追加が必要であることを視覚化する
Image type: concept diagram
Composition: square 1:1 illustration. Show a simple left-to-right transformation: on the left, a plain fast AI output represented by a generic screen block labeled 「速い出力」; in the center, a small filter/lens labeled 「案件理解」; on the right, a refined proposal mock represented by a clean screen block labeled 「提案材料」. Use 3 simple groups with plenty of white space and a small yellow accent only on the center filter.
Labels: 「速い出力」「案件理解」「提案材料」
Style: Create a clean n2p proposal slide illustration on a pure white background. Use black and dark gray linework, simple gray fills, and very small n2p yellow accents only. Avoid strong gradients, glossy surfaces, heavy shadows, decorative particles, excessive depth of field, and generic AI-looking decoration.
Content details: The visual should communicate that speed alone is not enough; the output becomes valuable only after intent and project-specific thinking are added. Use simple vector-like linework, no border, no decorative background.
Avoid: Do not use photorealistic people, stock-photo style, manga style, hand-drawn sketch style, neon colors, vivid gradients, glassmorphism, glowing blobs, excessive shadows, busy backgrounds, clutter, tiny unreadable text, fake detailed UI screenshots, or decorative elements that do not explain the slide.
Output: clean illustration, no border, white background, ready to place in a PPTX slide.
