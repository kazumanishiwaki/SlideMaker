## Prompt

Create a polished n2p proposal slide illustration on a pure white background, horizontal 4:3. Use black and dark gray linework, simple gray fills, and very small n2p yellow accents only. Calm, minimal, business-oriented, suitable for a Japanese proposal deck.

Show a clean transformation without any text: left side has a large soft speech bubble containing abstract fragments and question-like marks, representing vague sensory feedback; center has a small yellow document/card acting as a conversion point; right side has three stacked rule cards with check marks, representing explicit shared rules. Use simple arrows and generous white space. Make it feel like a finished business illustration, not a rough diagram.

No words, no letters, no numbers, no readable UI text. Leave all labels to be added later as editable PowerPoint text.

Avoid photorealistic people, stock-photo style, manga style, hand-drawn sketch style, neon colors, vivid gradients, glassmorphism, glowing blobs, excessive shadows, busy backgrounds, clutter, fake detailed UI screenshots, and decorative elements that do not explain the slide. No border.
